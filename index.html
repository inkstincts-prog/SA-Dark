Our Process

How We Work

Every project follows a rigorous, collaborative process — from the very first conversation to the final walk-through. You're involved at every stage, every decision.

A deep-dive consultation to understand your vision, lifestyle, site context and aspirations. No templates. No assumptions. Only listening.

Experimental Studio

The
Laboratory

Where we experiment. Where constraints dissolve. Where architecture becomes installation — and installation becomes art.

01
Parametric Installation · Pondicherry Highway

Strings

Designed as an installation pavilion, the parametric structure spreads over the entrance area of Café Albatross. A tensile canopy of MS steel framework with dyed threads and neon strip lights — filtering sunlight into mesmerising shadow play while housing an alfresco dining amphitheatre beneath.

ScopeDesign · Detailing
TypePavilion Installation
MaterialMS Steel · Threads · Neon
02
Detail Design · MKB Nagar, Chennai

Not a Door

A hidden door embedded seamlessly into wooden flute wall panelling — a discreet portal to the walk-in-wardrobe, hiding in plain sight. Tapping into our childhood desire for a secret place, the value of this intervention lies in extraordinary craftsmanship and precise diagramming of the design.

ScopeDesign · Fabrication · Execution
TypeFurniture Detail
03
Renovation · Coimbatore

Mars 360

Factory vestibule renovation completed with a minimal budget by reusing site materials entirely. Pellet wood panels refurbished from packaging crates, perforated scrap metal sheets from a nearby automotive factory, and a dramatic vertical plant wall as the visual keynote of this ingenious revamp.

ScopeDesign · PMC
Area200 sq. ft
BudgetMinimal / Reused Materials
04
Competition Proposal · Hypothetical

Stadium

One of our first large-scale computational design attempts — the concept takes cues from the form and behaviour of coral reefs. The building's shell acts as a barrier, screening like coral protects fish below. The parametric façade with circular panels responds to the calculated distance of the sun path.

ScopeDesign · Detailing
TypeCompetition Entry
MethodCNC Wood · Laser-cut Metal
05
Fabrication · Kodaikanal, Tamil Nadu

Parametric Wave Table

A parametric façade that undulates to mimic the terrain of the mountains — aptly fitting the restaurant's visual landscape. 500 individual CNC-cut rubber wood pieces, driven by parametric coding to minimise material wastage, assembled on site by skilled technicians exactly as the designer envisioned.

ScopeDesign · Fabrication · Execution
Pieces500 CNC-cut
MaterialRubber Wood · Parametric CNC
